H bot Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how H bot handles information when you use the bot on Discord.

1. Information H bot Processes

H bot may process information provided by Discord that is necessary for its features, including:

- Discord user IDs
- Discord usernames
- Discord server IDs
- Discord channel IDs
- Message content in channels where H bot's moderation features are active

2. Message Content

H bot may read message content in designated channels, such as an #h channel, to determine whether a message follows the channel's rules.

Message content is processed for this purpose and is not permanently stored.

3. Leaderboard Data

H bot stores Discord usernames and related leaderboard statistics for the `hleaderboard` feature.

This information is used to display and maintain the H leaderboard. H bot does not use this information for advertising or sell it to third parties.

4. Data Retention

Message content is not permanently stored after it has been processed.

Usernames and leaderboard statistics may be stored for as long as they are needed to provide the leaderboard feature.

5. Data Retention

H bot does not intentionally retain message content after it has been processed unless required for a specific feature.

Other technical information may be retained when necessary for the operation of the bot.

6. Discord

H bot operates through Discord and is subject to Discord's platform and services. Discord may independently collect and process information according to its own Privacy Policy.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as H bot changes. The date at the top of this page will be updated when significant changes are made.

8. Contact

If you have questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or data handled by H bot, please contact the H bot developer through the official support contact provided for the bot.
